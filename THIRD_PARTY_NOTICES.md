do not override upstream rights.

The retained source records and original source manifests distinguish
quoted material from author-written checks and engineering interpretations.
Full third-party research papers and web-page copies without an established
redistribution grant were excluded from the experiment archive; their
source identities and digests remain in CURATION_REPORT.json and archived
source-index records. Source URLs identify where to obtain the original work.

## External OPA prerequisite

The public archive does not contain an OPA binary. `revision/TOOLS.json`
records the official v1.19.0 static asset URL and historical SHA-256. The
matching official release digest establishes its source despite the embedded
`+dirty` marker. Users obtain it from upstream before offline replay.
OPA's Apache-2.0 license text is included for reference. Regal and the
historical API transport were excluded. The original local review archive
and its historical verification records are preserved.
